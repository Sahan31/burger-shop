Our Food Restaurant

Founded in 2012 in the vibrant heart of Colombo, Sarab began as a small passion kitchen with a big dream — to serve exceptional food that brings Sri Lankans together. Today, we're proud to serve thousands of happy food lovers across Colombo every week with the freshest local ingredients and the same warm hospitality that started it all.

100% Fresh Local Ingredients

We source daily from trusted local Sri Lankan farms and markets for maximum freshness and flavor.

Award-Winning Culinary Flavors

Recognized among Sri Lanka's top fast food and casual dining restaurants 5 years running.

Lightning-Fast Colombo Delivery

Order online and receive hot, fresh food delivered straight to your door across Colombo in under 25 minutes.
